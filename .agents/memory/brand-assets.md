Use a restrained type-only wordmark in warm ink and muted rosewood; do not use the older bright-pink illustrated logo. Use coded line icons for service shortcuts.

**Why:** the client said the supplied logo looked too pink and requested a calmer, less artificial presentation.

**How to apply:** keep the header and footer wordmark typographic, and use simple non-photographic icons for service shortcuts.

Do not display AI-generated or stock imagery as Glam by Dia portfolio work. Use only client-approved real portfolio photos; until those are supplied, use image-free layouts and an honest gallery placeholder.

**Why:** the client requested that all AI pictures be updated, and the project does not yet contain verified real client portfolio photos.

**How to apply:** avoid generated and unrelated third-party images in public-facing pages, especially where visitors could mistake them for actual Glam by Dia work.

Use one flat #fcdee3 site background with no floral or watercolor decoration. Keep service and booking actions responsive and prevent horizontal page overflow on mobile.

**Why:** the client explicitly rejected the leaf and flower background artwork and requested a single background color.

**How to apply:** set the page canvas to #fcdee3 and reserve other colors for readable text, cards, borders and controls.
